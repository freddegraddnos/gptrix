From Think it to Ink it.

InkSimulator helps people discover tattoos, try designs and intended size and placement in AR, find approved studios and send enquiries. Studios can show their work, manage conversations, sketches and bookings, and connect Fortnox for supported transactions.

## Why join?

- Your public studio profile and portfolio give clients another way to find your work and contact you. Search ranking and bookings are not guaranteed.
- Clients can explore the look, size and placement on their own body before visiting. The artist confirms final measurements and suitability in person.
- Keep references, sketches, customer conversations, available appointment times and studio notifications together.
- Google Calendar can reflect busy times. Fortnox integration can reduce manual entry for supported transactions; a Fortnox subscription is separate and books still need reconciliation.
- An approved studio can enable its own booking agent. Depending on the studio setting, a booking is confirmed immediately or awaits approval. The client must confirm that they are at least 18.

## Studio pricing

Free trial throughout 2026 with no fixed studio fee or commission. The Fortnox integration and Posture Pro are included in the studio plan. Fortnox itself requires a separate subscription. See https://www.inksimulator.com/pricing for current terms. VAT treatment is not stated here because it has not been verified.
